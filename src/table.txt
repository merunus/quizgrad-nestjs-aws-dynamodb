This is documentation for the single-table in my DynamoDB database


User element:
PK: USER#<UserId>
SK: #METADATA#<UserId>

Set element (owned by users):
PK: USER#<UserId> (to group sets by their owner)
SK: SET#<SetId>

Word element (belonging to sets):
PK: SET#<SetId> (to group words by their set)
SK: WORD#<WordId>
